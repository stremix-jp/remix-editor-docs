# 3. Playback, Seeking & Zoom

Before drawing any curves, get used to **moving to any position while listening and viewing at any zoom level**. You will repeat these operations hundreds of times during production.

## Play and pause

- The **Space** key or the play button in the footer toggles play/pause
- Space works no matter which panel is active (except while typing in a text field)

## Moving the playback position (seeking)

The playback position is shown as a vertical line (the playhead) in the curve editor. There are three ways to move it.

| Method | When to use |
|--------|-------------|
| Drag the seek slider in the footer | Rough moves |
| Click / drag in the playhead area at the top of the curve editor | Precise positioning within the visible range |
| Click a row in the Sections panel | Jump to the start of that scene (covered in lesson 5) |

## Zoom and pan

The visible range of the curve editor (the viewport) moves freely.

| Action | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Mouse wheel | Zoom in / out around the cursor |
| Right-drag | Move the visible range left / right (pan) |
| **J** key | Jump the viewport to the current playback position |
| **F** key | Toggle "follow playhead". When ON, the viewport follows during playback |
| "Fit all" on the toolbar | Return to a zoom level that shows the whole Remix |

If you lose the playhead while editing, press **J**.

## Changing playback speed

Click the speed display (1x) in the footer to choose from 0.25x to 2x. **Shift + >** / **Shift + <** also switch it.

- Drop to 0.5x or 0.75x when checking fine timing
- Use 1.5x or 2x to skim through the whole thing

## Repeating the same range (loop)

1. **Shift + drag** in the curve editor to select a time range
2. Turn on the loop button in the footer

While a selection exists, only that range repeats. Without a selection, the whole thing loops. The "Play from selection start" button in the footer starts playback at the beginning of the selection.

## Practice

Listen through the audio once, pause wherever the scene changes, and place the playhead there. Zoom in to position it precisely, press **J** to come back, and repeat.
